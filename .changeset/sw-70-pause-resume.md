Pause and Resume a workspace (SW-70). `werkmeister pause` and the `pause` MCP tool freeze the workspace's running containers (`docker pause`, `-sandbox-install` included) and hold its Claude session: while `.werkmeister/paused.json` exists, the agent-state hook ends the turn at the next tool call and blocks new prompts. `werkmeister resume` and `resume` thaw exactly those containers, starting any stopped meanwhile, release the session and, when Pause cut a working turn, tell it to continue: headless through claude-send, a Desktop session through the Workspaces pane. Every card, `status` and `check-in` show `paused`, and a paused workspace is never a check-in candidate. `up` and `restart` refuse while paused; `down` ends the pause. The card bar on a running workspace is now `■ Stop · ⏸ Pause` with `↻ Restart` in ⋯; VS Code's Stop icon is now a square.
