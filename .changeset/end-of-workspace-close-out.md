Close-out is the one end-of-workspace verb (#160). `sandbox close-out <ws> --abandon "<reason>"` ends a workspace whose work won't ship: it records the reason, leaves the issue, pushes every branch and keeps them, then removes the workspace. Both outcomes list terminal Claude sessions still open in the workspace. The pane shows one end button per card: ✓ Close out once shipped, otherwise ✕ Abandon… with a reason field. After a close-out, the pane and the `close-out` MCP tool archive the workspace's desktop sessions. The `remove-workspace` MCP tool, the pane's ✕ Remove and VS Code's Remove Workspace are gone; VS Code has Close Out Workspace. `sandbox remove-workspace` still works for one release but prints a deprecation notice.

`closeOut(name, opts, deps)` now takes `{ abandon }` as its second argument, and `deps` moves to the third.
